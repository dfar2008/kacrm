# English user-guide illustrations

All 18 illustrations referenced by `docs/user-guide.en.md` are stored in this directory. They are English adaptations of the corresponding Chinese historical screenshots and architecture diagram, created using an image-editing tool. They are documentation illustrations, not live captures or evidence of current runtime behavior.

Five existing English illustrations (architecture, dashboard, schema builder, skills, and scheduled tasks) were copied from `assets/readme/en/`. The remaining thirteen were localized from `assets/user-guide/screens/`. Original Chinese assets remain in their existing locations and are used by the Chinese guide and HTML print edition.
