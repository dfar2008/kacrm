In particular, if you modify the program and let users interact with it remotely over a computer network, section 13 requires you to prominently offer those users access to the Corresponding Source of your version at no charge. Distribution also carries obligations under the license. The complete license controls over this summary.

## Separate commercial licensing

An alternative commercial license may be negotiated with the relevant KaCRM copyright holders for code they own or are authorized to relicense. A signed agreement may grant rights such as proprietary distribution or hosting a modified version without the AGPL source-offer obligations for the code covered by that agreement.

This document is an explanation of the licensing model, not a commercial license grant. Downloading the repository, paying for support, or submitting an inquiry does not grant an AGPL exception. The scope, covered versions, fees, support, and any source-disclosure exceptions must be expressly agreed in a separate written license.

For commercial licensing inquiries, contact the repository owner through [the maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/dfar2008). Do not post confidential contract details in public issues.

Contributions remain subject to their applicable licenses and copyright ownership. An AGPL contribution alone does not authorize proprietary relicensing. Contributions included in a commercial offering require the necessary separate permission from their copyright holders, or another applicable license allowing that use. No copyright assignment or commercial relicensing permission is implied by submitting a pull request.
